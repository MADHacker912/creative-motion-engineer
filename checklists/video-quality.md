# Checklist: Video Quality Assurance
- [ ] Frame rate is consistent (30fps or 60fps) across all assets.
- [ ] Audio loudness conforms to standard (-14 LUFS for web / streaming).
- [ ] Color space is verified (Rec.709 with legal range).
- [ ] Fast-start flags (`+faststart`) enabled on MP4 exports.
- [ ] Zero audio clipping or abrupt audio pop at cut points.
