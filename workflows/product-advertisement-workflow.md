# Workflow: Product Advertisement Video (15s/30s)

## Overview
Focused commercial video creation targeting high-conversion social media and product hero spaces.
1. **The Hook (0-3s)**: High-speed macro shot, kinetic typography statement, bass riser.
2. **Feature Cascade (3-20s)**: 3 core product benefits, rhythmic sync to music transients.
3. **Hero Payoff (20-30s)**: Full product lockup, price/availability, decisive call to action.
