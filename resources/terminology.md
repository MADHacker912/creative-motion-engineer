# Encyclopedic Terminology of Creative Motion Engineering

### 1. Motion & Animation
- **Anticipation**: A preparatory motion in the opposite direction before the primary action.
- **Cubic Bezier**: A parametric curve defined by 4 control points governing velocity over time.
- **Damping Ratio ($\zeta$)**: Mathematical parameter describing how oscillations decay in spring physics.
- **FLIP**: First, Last, Invert, Play — high-performance layout transition technique.
- **Follow Through**: Uncompleted motion continuing after the main action has stopped.
- **Jitter / Stutter**: Frame rate drops causing visible jumpiness in animation.
- **Kinetic Typography**: Expressive, animated text moving to convey rhythm or emotion.
- **Scrubbing**: Tying the progress of an animation timeline directly to scroll or pointer position.
- **Squash and Stretch**: Distorting an object's dimensions to convey speed, momentum, and elasticity.
- **Stagger**: Offsetting the start times of identical animations across a list of elements.

### 2. 3D & CGI
- **Albedo**: The base color of a PBR material under neutral white light without shadows.
- **Ambient Occlusion (AO)**: Soft contact shadows appearing in crevices where light is occluded.
- **Draco Compression**: Open-source library for compressing 3D geometric meshes.
- **Draw Call**: A command issued by the CPU to the GPU to render a set of primitives.
- **Environment Map (HDRI)**: High Dynamic Range panoramic image used to calculate ambient lighting and reflections.
- **Frustum Culling**: Discarding objects outside the camera's field of view before rendering.
- **glTF / GLB**: Graphics Library Transmission Format — the standard runtime 3D format for the web.
- **InstancedMesh**: Rendering multiple identical meshes with varying transforms in a single draw call.
- **Normal Map**: An RGB texture encoding surface normals to simulate high-frequency geometric detail.
- **PBR (Physically Based Rendering)**: Shading model simulating real-world light physics (conservation of energy).
- **Roughness**: Parameter defining microscopic surface irregularities affecting specular highlight spread.

### 3. Shaders & VFX
- **Bloom**: Optical effect where bright highlights bleed beyond their physical boundaries.
- **Chromatic Aberration**: Optical distortion where lenses fail to focus all colors to the same convergence point.
- **Curl Noise**: Divergence-free noise vector field used for fluid and particle simulations.
- **Fragment Shader**: GPU program determining the final color and depth of a screen pixel.
- **GLSL**: OpenGL Shading Language used in WebGL.
- **Uniform**: Global variable passed from CPU to vertex and fragment shaders.
- **Varying**: Variable passed from vertex shader to fragment shader, smoothly interpolated across primitives.
- **Vertex Shader**: GPU program calculating screen coordinates and normal vectors for 3D vertices.

### 4. Video & Audio
- **Audio Ducking**: Automatically lowering background music volume when speech or primary sound occurs.
- **Frame Rate (FPS)**: The frequency of consecutive images displayed per second (24, 30, or 60).
- **Hit Point**: The exact frame where visual action aligns with an audio transient.
- **J-Cut**: Video editing transition where audio from the next scene begins before the visual cut.
- **L-Cut**: Video editing transition where audio from the previous scene continues into the new visual shot.
- **LUT (Lookup Table)**: Mathematical transform applied to color grade digital video footage.
- **ProRes**: Apple's intermediate video codec providing visually lossless intra-frame compression.
- **Transient**: High-energy, short-duration peak at the beginning of an audio waveform (e.g., drum hit, snap).
