# Domain: 3D Animation, Real-Time WebGL & CGI

## 1. Scope & Domain Objectives
Bridge the gap between cinematic CGI and real-time interactive 3D web experiences:
- High-fidelity 3D modeling and low-poly retopology for WebGL.
- Physically Based Rendering (PBR) materials, lighting, and HDR studio environments.
- Cinematic camera choreographies, orbital dampening, and dolly tracks.
- Luxury product visualization and turntable presentations.
- Extreme scene optimization (draw call batching, KTX2/Draco compression, memory disposal).

---

## 2. Sub-Modules
- `modelling.md`: Poly-count budgets, quad topology, normal baking, beveling.
- `materials-lighting.md`: PBR parameters, studio three-point setups, HDR probes.
- `camera-animation.md`: FOV, camera rigs, smooth pointer damping, bounding box fits.
- `product-visualization.md`: Turntable reveals, internal assembly, glass & caustics.
- `scene-optimization.md`: InstancedMesh, texture mipmapping, draw call budgeting.
