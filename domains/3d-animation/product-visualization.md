# Product Visualization & Interactive Showcases

## 1. Architectural Anatomy of a 3D Product Page
1. **Initial Framing**: Clean camera profile displaying the full product silhouette against a calm, branded neutral backdrop.
2. **Exploded View Animation**: Sequential translation of internal sub-assemblies along their natural mechanical axes.
3. **Material Swapping**: Dynamic texture and color changes with smooth cross-fading rather than instant popping.
4. **Interactive Hotspots**: 3D-to-2D projected annotations that track real mesh vertices as the camera rotates.
