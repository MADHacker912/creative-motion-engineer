# Storyboarding: Shot Planning and Visual Directing

## 1. Standard Storyboard Shot Specifications
Every planned shot must specify:
1. **Shot ID**: Sequence and shot number (`SEQ01_SH02`).
2. **Framing**: Extreme Wide (EWS), Wide (WS), Medium (MS), Close-Up (CU), Extreme Close-Up (ECU).
3. **Camera Movement**: Static, Track In (Dolly In), Orbital Sweep, Crane Up.
4. **Duration**: Timecode in frames (`@30fps` or `@60fps`).
5. **Visual Focus**: The primary actor or UI component in frame.
6. **Sound Cue**: Audio transient, voiceover line, or SFX impact.

---

## 2. Markdown Storyboard Template

```markdown
### Sequence 01: The Problem Space
- **Shot 01 (0.0s - 2.5s) [CU]**: Fast macro zoom into complex wireframe chaos. Monochromatic deep blue. Sound: Low sub rumble with rising electric static.
- **Shot 02 (2.5s - 4.0s) [WS]**: Central monolithic emblem slams into center frame, clearing the noise with a radial shockwave. Sound: Heavy cinematic bass impact + glass resonance.
- **Shot 03 (4.0s - 7.5s) [MS]**: UI workspace folds open in 3D perspective, fluid animations gliding into place. Sound: Crisp mechanical clicks and light atmospheric synth pad.
```
