# Storytelling: Narrative Pacing for High-Impact Video

## 1. The 3-Act Commercial Structure (15s & 30s Formats)

In digital product advertising, viewers decide to skip within the first **3 seconds**. The structure must be aggressive yet emotionally satisfying:

```
[Act 1: The Hook & Agitation (0 - 3s)]
 └─► A striking statement or visceral problem representation. Rapid cuts, high contrast.

[Act 2: The Transformation & Core Showcase (3 - 22s)]
 └─► The solution revealed. Fluid product interaction, kinetic benefits, architectural beauty.

[Act 3: The Climax & Final Grounding (22 - 30s)]
 └─► Ultimate value proposition, hero lockup, memorable audio sting, unmistakable CTA.
```

---

## 2. Visual Rhythm & Pacing Curves
- **Accelerando**: Gradually decreasing shot durations (e.g. 2s ➔ 1.2s ➔ 0.8s ➔ 0.4s) to build adrenaline before a product drop.
- **Ritardando**: Extending a shot's duration (e.g. holding a beauty shot for 3.5s) to allow a monumental revelation to register emotionally.
