# Domain: Cinematic Video Production & Programmatic Rendering

## 1. Scope & Capabilities
Teach the agent to produce broadcast-grade cinematic videos, promotional teasers, software commercials, and social media reels:
- Narrative structuring and visual storyboarding.
- Programmatic video rendering via React and Remotion.
- Multi-track sound design, audio stems, and hit-point synchronization.
- Color grading, ACES/Rec.709 color spaces, and film look LUT application.
- Production-grade FFmpeg encoding pipelines.

---

## 2. Sub-Modules
- `storytelling.md`: Narrative pacing, 3-act digital commercials, emotional hooks.
- `storyboarding.md`: Shot lists, framing guides, camera directions.
- `cinematic-editing.md`: Rhythmic editing, L/J cuts, match cuts, montage theory.
- `sound-design.md`: Stems, ducking, transient alignment, whooshes & risers.
- `color-grading.md`: Lift/Gamma/Gain, curves, saturation control, cinematic LUTs.
- `video-export.md`: Codecs (H.264, H.265, ProRes), bitrates, aspect ratios.
