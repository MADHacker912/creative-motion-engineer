# Domain: Motion Graphics & Brand Animation

## 1. Scope & Domain Objectives
Motion graphics transforms static design assets into dynamic, communicative visual systems. Within this domain, you will master:
- Expressive kinetic typography and letterform choreography.
- Vector logo reveals, morphing brand stings, and animated design tokens.
- Seamless narrative transitions (whip pans, match cuts, mask wipes).
- Procedural and physics-based particle systems.
- High-clarity animated infographics, data visualizations, and dynamic charts.

---

## 2. Core Technical Architecture
- **Primary Engines**: GSAP (GreenSock), Remotion, SVG / SMIL, After Effects (reference), Lottie, Rive.
- **Delivery Formats**: Transparent WebM, Lottie JSON, Rive `.riv`, MP4 (H.264), Animated SVG, WebGL Canvas.

---

## 3. Sub-Module Guide
- `kinetic-typography.md`: Deep dive into character, word, and line choreography.
- `logo-animation.md`: Geometric stroke reveals, shape morphs, and logo lockups.
- `transitions.md`: Scene wipes, camera pushes, and mask transitions.
- `particle-systems.md`: 2D/3D particle emitters, turbulence, and physics.
- `infographic-animation.md`: Animated counters, dynamic bar charts, and data flow.
