# Domain: Visual Effects (VFX) & Custom Shaders

## 1. Scope & Domain Objectives
Create high-impact digital illusions and GPU post-processing effects:
- Compositing and alpha matte operations.
- Dynamic image distortion, heat waves, and chromatic aberration.
- GPU-accelerated particle compute simulations.
- Volumetric lighting and bloom passes.
- Custom fragment shaders (GLSL ES 3.0).
