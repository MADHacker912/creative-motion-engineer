# Professional Art Direction & Conceptual Pre-Production

## 1. The Pre-Production Mandate: Concept Before Code
In high-end design ateliers and VFX studios, opening 3D software before articulating a clear creative thesis is considered an amateur mistake.

Before generating any geometry or writing Blender Python scripts, the agent must execute this **10-Step Pre-Production Protocol**:

```
[1. Brief Analysis]     ──► Deconstruct client intent, core message & deliverable specs.
[2. Target Audience]    ──► Executive enterprise? Tech innovators? Luxury collectors?
[3. Emotional Anchor]   ──► Precision confidence? Atmospheric mystery? Kinetic vitality?
[4. Art Direction]      ──► Select from verified visual dialect matrix (Section 2).
[5. Color Discipline]   ──► Establish 60-30-10 palette (Base, Surface, Signal Accent).
[6. Material Palette]   ──► Select 2-3 tactile materials (e.g. Brushed Titanium + Smoked Glass).
[7. Composition Plan]   ──► Dynamic symmetry, golden ratio, or monumental monolith framing.
[8. Camera Language]    ──► Lens selection (85mm telephoto vs 28mm dynamic wide) and DoF.
[9. Light Strategy]     ──► Motivated lighting: large softbox, rim streak, or high-contrast chiaroscuro.
[10. Quality Benchmark] ──► Establish pass/fail criteria against Gate 1-9 production standards.
```

## 2. Art Direction Dialects Matrix

Never default to generic "cyberpunk neon" or template aesthetics. Choose an intentional style grounded in design history:

| Creative Dialect | Visual Traits | Material Palette | Lighting Style | Camera Language |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **High-End Luxury Product** | Disciplined symmetry, vast negative space, micro-beveled edges. | Brushed platinum, satin ceramic, sapphire crystal glass. | Large overhead softbox + continuous razor-sharp strip rim. | 85mm–105mm telephoto, shallow DoF (`f/2.8`), subtle orbit. |
| **Monolithic Brutalist** | Heavy stone mass, sharp angular silhouettes, exposed structural seams. | Cast concrete, raw anodized aluminum, obsidian slate. | Low-angle harsh key light, deep shadows, subtle volume haze. | 28mm wide-angle, low camera elevation looking upward. |
| **Clean Swiss Minimalist** | Asymmetrical grid alignment, pristine surfaces, high value contrast. | Matte white polymer, frosted glass, polished stainless steel. | High-key soft ambient fill, zero harsh shadows, pure background. | 50mm natural perspective, orthographic or flat front profiles. |
| **Organic Futuristic** | Fluid double-curved surfaces, parametric ribbon lattices. | Iridescent clearcoat, translucent bio-resin, warm liquid gold. | Soft colorful rim accents, gradient background falloff. | 70mm lens, continuous flowing spline paths. |

## 3. Visual Reference Deconstruction Protocol
When analyzing real-world reference imagery or client moodboards:
1. **Trace Specular Glints**: Where do bright edge glints appear? This reveals the exact positions and sizes of the studio lights.
2. **Examine Shadow Penumbra**: Are shadow edges sharp or soft? Soft shadows indicate large diffused light sources; sharp shadows indicate small point sources.
3. **Analyze Material Roughness**: Notice how specular reflections blur across surfaces. Identify where handling and micro-imperfections break the highlight.
4. **Determine Lens Compression**: Look at horizontal and vertical convergence. Parallel vertical lines indicate an 85mm+ telephoto lens or shift-lens framing.
